Sorry, We're Here for Eternity

1974 · Articles

Soon it will be possible to extend human life indefinitely. After thousands of years of desperate struggle against death and deepest anguish at its inevitability there is at last .hope of winning that struggle. In research centers around the world efforts are now accelerating to overcome aging and, in time, death itself.

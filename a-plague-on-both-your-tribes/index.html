A Plague on Both Your Tribes

1972 · Articles

The Arab-Israeli quarrel is developing into a monumental bore. While East and West Europeans, North and South Koreans, Indians and Pakistanis, American and Russians, Chinese and Japanese are all rapidly patching up their differences, the Arabs and Israelis, acting like adolescents, refuse to resolve their wasteful 25-year-old brawl.
